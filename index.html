Our Mission & Vision

The Sandra Bullocks Orphanage Foundation was established with a simple yet powerful mission: to provide orphaned children with a safe, loving home and the opportunities they need to thrive.

For over a decade, we have been committed to transforming the lives of orphaned children by providing:

  • Safe and nurturing residential care
  • Quality education and learning resources
  • Comprehensive healthcare and nutrition
  • Emotional support and counseling
  • Life skills training for future independence

Our vision is a world where every orphaned child has access to love, care, and the opportunity to reach their full potential. To date, we have cared for over 500 children, with many going on to successful careers, higher education, and loving adoptive families.
